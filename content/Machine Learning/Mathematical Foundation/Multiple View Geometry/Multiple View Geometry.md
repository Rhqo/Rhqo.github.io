The world is 3D, and vision systems capture only 2D images. \
Understanding the projection of the 3D world into 2D images, and how to reverse this projection to recover the 3D structure of the scene, is one of the most important topics in the study of vision (both natural vision and computer vision). \
Geometry is therefore a fundamental tool in computer vision.
